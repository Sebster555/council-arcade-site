Privacy Policy

Council Arcade · Last updated September 30, 2026

The short version

Council Arcade collects nothing. It works offline, shows no ads, and does no tracking.

What the app collects

Nothing. Council Arcade:

  • Has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.
  • Uses no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking tools of any kind.
  • Makes no network requests. All game content ships inside the app.
  • Does not access your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, or any other device permission.
  • Uses no cookies or tracking identifiers.

Data stored on your device

Game progress such as high scores is saved only in the app's local storage on your device. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If a future version ever adds network features or data collection, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released.
