Council Arcade

Thirteen neon arcade cabinets in one little app for iPhone.

Dodge, glide, stack, defend, and patch your way through a night shift of quick arcade games starring a small robot crew.

  • 13 games, from reflex runners to a tower-defense and a cable puzzle
  • Works completely offline — no internet connection needed
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts
  • Collects no data at all
