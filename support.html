Support

Get help or report a bug

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page: github.com/Sebster555/council-arcade-site/issues, or email proton.cupcake_7c@icloud.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and which game you were playing.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection? No. Every game runs fully offline.

Where are my high scores saved? Only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Does the app collect my data? No. See the privacy policy.
